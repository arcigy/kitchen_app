### P0-2: make application files durable

Develop is persistent. Required target:

- choose CapRover persistent volume or S3-compatible object storage;
- copy first, verify checksums and tenant paths, switch reads/writes, then retain old data for rollback;
- test a redeploy and restore before deleting any old copy.

No file may be deleted during migration without a separate explicit approval.

Develop passed a full second deployment with an identical marker hash. P0-2 has no live source-copy step left; its remaining release gate is the production empty-volume mount plus post-redeploy checksum proof.
